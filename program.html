Program

Analyst program

A one-semester, model-driven curriculum running February through April. Every session is built around a model members work through.

Draft topics · Not final session titles

  1. Discount rates in EMs: local versus hard currency, country risk premia
  2. Sovereign credit and how sovereign risk passes through to other assets
  3. FX market structure, capital controls, and hedging
  4. Currency mismatch: firms with dollar debt and local-currency revenue
  5. Governance and promoter-controlled equity
  6. Liquidity in non-US markets
  7. Sovereign restructurings
  8. Securitization and structured credit in EMs

Capstone

Each member chooses one format and presents it in mid-April.

Research paper

A real research question about a specific market, a thesis, and the data to defend it.

Stock pitch

An equity valuation with its inputs adjusted for the market the company operates in.

Bond pitch

A view on sovereign or corporate credit, built on the fixed income and credit work from the program.
