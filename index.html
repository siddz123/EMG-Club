What we do

EMG is a technical, model-driven club that teaches students to invest and deploy capital in emerging markets, through public markets or otherwise. Every session is built around a model that members work through.

We are not a discussion club. We assume members already know the fundamentals, such as what a central bank does, and build from there.

Two halves

The club has two halves. Much of the value is where they meet.

Finance

Valuation, and how its inputs change in emerging markets.

  • Local versus hard-currency discount rates
  • Country and sovereign risk
  • Valuing a foreign company in local currency
  • Fixed income and structured credit

Economics and research

Asking a real question about a specific market and answering it.

  • Forming a research question
  • Building a thesis
  • Defending it with data

The program

Analyst program

A one-semester, model-driven curriculum, February through April.

Capstone

A research paper, stock pitch or bond pitch, presented in mid-April.
