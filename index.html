Emerging Markets Group at IU

We teach emerging markets through two lenses, macro and finance. The methods are the same as in US markets. Only the inputs change, and we learn to adjust them correctly.

Launching Spring 2027. First meeting date coming soon.

What we do

The club runs two tracks, each with regular sessions. Both come together in the capstone.

Track 1

Econ & Macro Outlook

  • Central bank policy
  • Currencies
  • Fiscal policy

Goal: form a question or thesis about a country, and answer it.

Track 2

Finance

  • Valuation
  • Hard vs. local currency
  • Capital markets
  • Liquidity

Goal: value any asset or investment outside the US, whether you're investing in a company, project or instrument, or lending to a company, project or government.

Capstone

Case competition

Both tracks combine here. Members pair a country thesis with a valuation and defend it.

Example thesis “Indonesia’s capital controls negatively affect its bond market.”
